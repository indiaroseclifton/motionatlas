The machine makes its own noise.

Six small, precise sounds: ticks, clicks, chirps and a buzz. All six are synthesised live in the browser, so there are no audio files to load. Sound is off until a person turns it on, and the choice is remembered on their device.

  • Engine Web Audio API
  • Files 0 KB
  • Default off

Play the kit

Pressing play turns interface sound on for this site. Use the switch to turn it off again; it is the same setting every 01 surface reads.

Off
  • Tick

    Hover over anything interactive, and every new line of a feed.

    NOISE · HIGH-PASS 5.2kHz · 12ms
  • Click

    Any press of a button or link.

    SINE · 2.4kHz · 30ms
  • Chirp

    Something new arrived: a model, an agent, a commit, a toast.

    SINE · 3.2 → 6.4kHz SWEEP · 60ms
  • Confirm

    A stage passed, a task done, a run complete.

    SINE · 1.6kHz THEN 2.4kHz · 55ms APART
  • Buzz

    Blocked, refused, failed. Paired with an error toast.

    SAW · 118Hz · BAND-PASS 900Hz · 90ms
  • Deny

    A field needs fixing before the form can continue.

    SQUARE · 220 → 180Hz · 80ms

API

O1.Sound.on            // true when the person has switched sound on
O1.Sound.set(true)     // switch on (remembered in localStorage 'o1-sound'), plays confirm
O1.Sound.tick()        // each call is silent while sound is off
O1.Sound.click(); O1.Sound.chirp(); O1.Sound.confirm(); O1.Sound.buzz(); O1.Sound.deny();

Do and don't

Do

  • Keep sound off by default and give it a visible switch with aria-pressed or role="switch".
  • Pair every sound with a visible change. Sound confirms; it never informs alone.
  • Keep peaks low: the kit never goes above 0.07 gain.
  • Turn sound off by default in Workspace, where people work for hours.

Don't

  • Autoplay any sound before a person has interacted.
  • Add melodies, voices, music or sampled effects.
  • Play a sound on scroll, on every keystroke, or on a timer.
  • Use buzz for anything that is not a real failure.
