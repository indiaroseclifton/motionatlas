Earn your place in the system.

Most ideas should be a pattern, not a component. A new component has to be needed in at least two products, impossible to build from what exists, and as accessible as everything already here.

  • Prefix u-
  • Review design + code + a11y

From idea to release

01 · PROPOSEDescribe the problem, the two places it is needed, and why existing parts fail.
02 · PROTOTYPEBuild it in a real screen using only tokens. Screenshots of both products.
03 · REVIEWDesign, code and accessibility review against the checklist below.
04 · DOCUMENTA section on its family page: examples, code, anatomy, rules, keyboard.
05 · RELEASEMinor version bump and a changelog line in the house voice.
