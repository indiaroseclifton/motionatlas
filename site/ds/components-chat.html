Anatomy

  1. User message.u-msg.user: right, 17–21px platinum.
  2. Who.who: mono caps and a glyph.
  3. Route.u-route: the models, lit as they lock.
  4. Tool call.u-tool: tool in cyan, result, green tick.
  5. Body.body: the answer, lead sentence in platinum.
  6. Proof lineMono caps: time, models, agents, tools.

Do

  • Lead the answer with the result in <strong>.
  • End every answer with the proof line.

Don't

  • Wrap messages in bubbles or cards.
  • Show avatars of people for agents; use glyphs.
