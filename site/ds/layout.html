Asymmetric on purpose.

A 4px base, generous gutters and a 12-column grid that is never used symmetrically. Content leans left, live data sits off to the right, and nothing is mirrored down the middle.

  • Base unit 4px
  • Gutter max(20px, 4.2vw)
  • Rows 48 · 40 · 32

Space scale

Eight steps on a 4px base. Inside components use 4–16; between components 16–32; between sections 48–96 and up. If a gap is not on this list, it is wrong.

The grid

Twelve columns with 16px gaps inside a fluid gutter. Headlines take columns 1–8, body 1–6, and live data docks into 9–12. The empty right-of-centre space is the point: it is where the city shows through.

The best agents.

They build the next ones. Columns 1 to 6 for copy, never wider than 70 characters.

Breakpoints

Four, chosen where the real layouts break rather than at device sizes. Design mobile first, check at 390px wide, and never let the page scroll sideways.

Do and don't

Do

  • Give every grid and flex child that holds text min-width: 0.
  • Let tables and code scroll inside their own container, never the page.
  • Keep one density per surface.
  • Leave the right of centre open on hero layouts.

Don't

  • Centre a layout on its vertical axis or mirror it.
  • Box content in cards to separate it; use space and a hairline.
  • Mix 32px and 48px rows on one screen.
  • Invent spacing values between the scale steps.
