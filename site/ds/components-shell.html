App shell

232px side navigation, a 52px sticky top bar with search and one primary action, and a page that scrolls on its own. Under 900px the side navigation becomes a drawer behind the menu button. Resize your window to see it switch; this example runs in its own frame.

Anatomy

  1. Side.u-side: sticky, full height, brand, sections, foot with status.
  2. Top bar.u-top: 52px, blurred graphite, burger on small screens.
  3. Page.u-page: single-column grid, 20px gap, children min-width:0.
  4. Burger.u-burger: toggles .nav-open on .u-app.

Do

  • Keep one primary action in the top bar per view.
  • Show system status at the foot of the side navigation.

Don't

  • Nest more than two levels in the side navigation.
  • Hide search; every view answers to ⌘ K.

Dense modifier

Add .u-dense to any container and every row inside drops from 40px to 32px with tighter padding. Workspace sets it on the whole app.
