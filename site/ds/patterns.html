App shell

Every 01 tool, from the Console to Workspace, opens into the same frame: navigation on the left, one top bar, a page that scrolls on its own. People learn it once.

  • Left is where you are: sections, counts, status at the foot.
  • Top is what you can do here: breadcrumbs, search, one primary action.
  • Page is the work: a heading in Geist, then frames for live data and panels for groups.
