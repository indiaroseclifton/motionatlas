Six principles

Every decision on every page in this system traces back to one of these. When two of them disagree, the earlier one wins.

  1. Hairlines, not boxes.Structure comes from 1px lines and corner ticks. A filled card is the exception, never the default, and callout cards do not exist.
  2. Signal is scarce.Cyan marks the one thing that matters on screen. Green means live. Amber and purple are rare enough that you notice them.
  3. Machines speak mono.Anything the system says, from status to logs to numbers, is Geist Mono in capitals. People get Geist.
  4. Arrive along a route.Motion travels a trace and locks into place. Nothing floats, bounces or fades for decoration.
  5. Numbers tick.Values change in visible steps, with a tick when sound is on. Smooth tweens hide what changed.
  6. Never small, never shy.Headlines are huge, left and off-centre. Screens are asymmetric; nothing is mirrored, nothing is hedged.

Quick start

The system is plain CSS and a small script. No build step, no framework, no package manager. Link the four hosted files and write HTML.

<link rel="stylesheet" href="https://motionatlas-ten.vercel.app/work/01/01.css">
<link rel="stylesheet" href="https://motionatlas-ten.vercel.app/work/01/01-ui.css">

<script src="https://motionatlas-ten.vercel.app/work/01/01.js"></script>
<script src="https://motionatlas-ten.vercel.app/work/01/01-ui.js"></script>
<script>UI.wire()</script>

One system, three volumes

The tokens, components and motion are identical everywhere. What changes is the volume: how big the type gets, how dense the rows are, how loud the motion plays.

Showcase · loudThe public 01ai site. Display type to 132px, the city circuits hero, decode on every headline, 48px rows.
Agency · sellsCustomer-facing. Same scale, fewer effects, every claim backed by a number. Standard 40px rows.
Workspace · denseThe HUD people live in all day. Add .u-dense for 32px rows, quiet motion, sound off by default.
