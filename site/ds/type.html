The scale

Seven tokens. The three headline sizes are fluid clamps, so a headline is always as big as the screen allows. Values below are read from 01.css.

Product scale

Inside the Console and Workspace, type runs tighter: titles at 18px, body at 13.5–15.5px, labels at 11–12px mono, numbers at 28px mono in stats. Component classes carry these sizes, so you rarely set them by hand.

Pairing

The pair works because they never do each other's job. A mono label sits above or beside a Geist value or heading; the two never mix inside one sentence.

Numerals

Numbers are the proof, so they get mono and tabular figures. When values tick, nothing around them moves.

  • Group thousands with commas: 38,940 not 38940.
  • Units follow without a space in mono: 38ms, 4.1s, 99.9%.
  • Align right in tables with .r and .u-num.
  • Never round a claim down. 1000+ models, not "around 1000".

Do and don't

Do

  • Set headlines big, left-aligned and tight: −0.03em to −0.055em tracking, 0.9–1.0 line height.
  • Use text-wrap: balance on headlines and keep them to four lines or fewer.
  • Keep body lines between 45 and 70 characters.
  • Track mono capitals open, 0.06–0.14em.

Don't

  • Set a page headline smaller than H2. Small headlines read as unsure.
  • Centre a headline or mirror a layout around it.
  • Use mono for sentences people read, or Geist for machine output.
  • Use italics, underlines for emphasis, or all-caps Geist.
