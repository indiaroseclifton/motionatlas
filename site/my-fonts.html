My fonts

Use a font you own in the brand generator, site builder, mockup studio and chart studio. Choose the files from your device; they are stored in this browser and nowhere else.

Licences and privacy

Fonts you add are your responsibility. The atlas never uploads, copies or redistributes them, and it does not read licence files. Check that your licence covers the use you plan, especially for exports that embed a font (ZIP files, SVG logos, published sites).

Fonts live in this browser's IndexedDB. Clearing site data removes them, and they do not follow you to another browser or device. In private windows they last for the page session only.
