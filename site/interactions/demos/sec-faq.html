Ask us
Do I need my own footage?

No. You can start from a recipe and a character bible; we generate the shots. If you have footage, upload it and we’ll match the grade.

Who owns what I make?

You do. Episodes, thumbnails and recipe cards are yours to post anywhere, including after you cancel.

How do recipe credits work?

When a recipe comes from a known chef or book, we add an on-screen credit and a line in the description, and hold the post until someone approves it.

Can my chef look the same every time?

Yes. A character bible fixes the face, hands, clothes and kitchen, and every shot is checked against it before it renders.

What happens if I cancel?

Your library stays downloadable for 90 days. We’ll email you before anything is deleted.
