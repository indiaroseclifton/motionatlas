Machine sounds.

Six small, precise interface sounds: ticks, buzzes and high pitches, synthesised live with the Web Audio API, so there are no audio files to load. Sound stays off until the visitor turns it on, and the choice is remembered.

  • Tick

    Hover, and every new line in a feed.

    NOISE · HP 5.2kHz · 12ms
  • Click

    Any press.

    SINE · 2.4kHz · 30ms
  • Chirp

    Something new arrived.

    SINE · 3.2→6.4kHz · 60ms
  • Confirm

    A step passed, a task done.

    SINE · 1.6 + 2.4kHz
  • Buzz

    Blocked, refused, failed.

    SAW · 118Hz · BP 900Hz · 90ms
  • Deny

    A field needs fixing.

    SQUARE · 220→180Hz · 80ms
