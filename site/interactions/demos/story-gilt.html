Gilt

An atelier of gold leaf · scroll

I · Flight

Beaten thinner than breath.

Each leaf starts as a coin of 23-carat gold, hammered until light passes through it. We lay it with a squirrel-hair brush and the warmth of a palm.

II · Line

Drawn, then drawn again.

Our figures are sketched in gesso, then gilded stroke by stroke. Where the leaf breaks, we leave it broken. The cracks catch the most light.

III · Fire

Something that refuses to dim.

Gold does not tarnish. A piece made today will look the same to whoever finds it in three hundred years.
