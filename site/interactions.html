Interactions shelf.

Seventeen showpiece interactions, four full scroll-story sites built around our films, eight page sections that switch between our four styles, and eleven Signal & data pieces for building a powerful AI or infrastructure site, each with a CONFIG block at the top to set your own colours, copy and data. Each is one HTML file, with no libraries except three.js for the circuit city. Open any piece to try it full size, copy its code, or copy a prompt that tells an AI builder how to recreate it in your own stack.

How to use these. Every piece works with a keyboard, has a phone layout and calms down when the visitor has asked for reduced motion. Copied code points its images at this site, so it runs as soon as you paste it; swap in your own images before you ship. The ideas here follow familiar patterns from interaction galleries; the designs, names and code are our own.
