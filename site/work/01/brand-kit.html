06 · Motion

Slow film, fast interface.

  • Film is slow. Ten-second loops, one camera move each, no cuts faster than a second.
  • Interface is fast. Lines draw in 900ms on the route ease, labels lock in at 320ms.
  • Numbers tick. Stats change in six visible steps, never a smooth tween.
  • Type types. The headline types once at 55ms a character, then the caret blinks.
  • Tabs advance. Every 4.2 seconds, with a progress rail, a pause button and a hold on focus.
  • Reduced motion. Films stay on their poster, lines are drawn, text is set, nothing loops.

07 · Voice

Scarcity, stated as fact.

The new concepts make scarcity part of the brag. "Not available" is a status, not an apology: it sits next to the numbers that prove demand. Still never "by invitation only", never a velvet-rope cliché.
