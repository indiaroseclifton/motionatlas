One system. Three volumes.

The interface system behind every 01 surface. The showcase plays it loud, the Agency site plays it to sell, and Workspace plays it dense. The tokens, components and motion are the same everywhere; only the density and the volume change.

  1. Hairlines, not boxes.Structure comes from 1px lines and corner ticks. A filled card is the exception, never the default.
  2. Signal is scarce.Cyan marks the one thing that matters on screen. Green means live. Amber and purple are rare.
  3. Machines speak mono.Anything the system says (status, logs, numbers, labels) is in Geist Mono caps. People get Geist.
  4. Arrive along a route.Motion travels a trace and locks into place. Nothing floats, bounces or fades for decoration.
  5. Numbers tick.Values change in visible steps, with a tick when sound is on. Smooth tweens hide what changed.
  6. Never small, never shy.Headlines are huge, left and off-centre. Screens are asymmetric; nothing is mirrored.

Colour roles

Use roles, not raw values. Contrast is measured against the page (graphite 950).

Use cyan for focus, the active route and the primary signal.

Use cyan for body text or large fills.

Use green only for live, healthy or complete.

Use green for buttons or decoration.

Keep amber for warnings and the occasional accent.

Put purple and amber on the same screen as decoration.

Type

Geist for people, Geist Mono for the machine. Showcase headlines run up to 132px; Workspace runs a tighter scale.

Space & density

A 4px base. Each surface picks a row height: Showcase breathes, Console is standard, Workspace is dense.

Icons & glyphs

One 24px set, 1.5px stroke, square caps and mitred corners, so they match the hard-edged type. Every agent, model and person also gets a generated glyph: a mirrored 5×5 grid of bits, unique to its name.

Motion & sound

Route, lock, decode and tick. Live demos, easing tokens and the six synthesised sounds are in the brand book. In product UI, keep motion under 320ms and never block input.

Decode headlines and new agent names once, on arrival.

Decode body text, labels or anything the user is reading.

Tick counters in six steps, and play the tick sound only when sound is on.

Animate layout while a person is typing or choosing.

Make every effect instant under reduced motion.

Loop motion in someone's peripheral vision without a pause control.

Buttons

A cyan trace draws across the top on hover. Platinum (solid) for the one primary action per view; signal for the hero action in a run; line for everything else.

Fields

Labels sit above, always visible. Errors sit under the field and say what to do. Showcase forms use the underline style (o-input); product UI uses these boxed fields.

Shown in logs and the swarm.

Choices

Checkboxes and radios for forms, switches for settings that apply at once, a segmented control for 2–4 views, and ranges for continuous values with the number shown.

App shell

232px side navigation, a 52px sticky top bar with search (⌘K) and status, and a page that scrolls on its own. Under 900px the side navigation becomes a drawer. The Console, Workspace and every future 01 tool use this same shell.

Keep one primary action in the top bar per view.

Nest more than two levels in the side navigation.

Show system status (live dot, version) at the foot of the side navigation.

Hide search; every view answers to ⌘K.

The agent run

Every request moves through the same five visible stages, so people learn to read a run at a glance.

01 · ROUTEModels lock on along a trace, one per role.
02 · SPAWNThe swarm grid lights as agents start.
03 · ACTTool calls stream in, each with its tool's mark and a green tick.
04 · ANSWERThe answer streams word by word behind a cyan cursor.
05 · PROOFOne closing line: time, models, agents, tools.

Forms

Validate on Continue, not on every keystroke. Focus the first problem, write the fix in the error, and keep drafts so nobody loses work. The full pattern is in the 01ai application.
