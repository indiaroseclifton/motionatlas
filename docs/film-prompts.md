# Film prompts for the remaining 25 specimens

These match the 11 films already in the atlas: **1168 × 784 (3:2), 24 fps, 15 seconds, no audio**,
photographic, a locked-off or very slow camera, no text, captions, logos or watermarks.

**Start frame:** use the entry's existing render as the first frame (image-to-video). The renders are
in `site/assets/specimens/` (for example `a41-1.webp` for A41), or the originals from your package.

**Loop:** each film is trimmed and crossfaded into a seamless 14-second loop when it's added, so it
helps if the last two seconds look like the first.

Entries marked *(optional)* already have a live simulation in the animation studio. A film still
helps for the catalogue card, but it isn't needed.

When you have a clip, save it as the entry code (`A41.mp4`) and either send it to me or run
`tools/add-film.sh A41 path/to/A41.mp4`, which encodes the loop and poster and adds it to the
entry, the card preview and the film library.

### A41 · Domino / chain-reaction cascade
Start frame `a41-1.webp`. A curved line of dark wooden dominoes on a slate tabletop in low raking light. The first domino tips and the fall travels smoothly along the whole curve from left to right, each tile clicking into the next, soft shadows sweeping with them. In the final two seconds a fresh, standing line is revealed by a slow dissolve so the loop restarts. Locked camera at tabletop height, shallow depth of field.

### A47 · Color cycling / palette animation
Start frame `a47-1.webp`. A layered, flat-colour landscape of rolling bands. The shapes never move; only the colours cycle, sunset magenta and orange flowing downhill through the bands like slow water, and the sky shifting from gold to violet. Smooth, continuous, no flicker. Locked camera.

### A50 · Thin-film interference (soap)
Start frame `a50-1.webp`. Macro of a large curved soap film against black. Iridescent bands of magenta, gold, cyan and green drift and swirl slowly as the film drains downward; thin black spots bloom near the top. Glossy reflections stay continuous. Locked camera, very slow motion.

### A51 · Mycelial network growth
Start frame `a51-1.webp`. Time-lapse macro of fine white mycelium spreading across dark, crumbly soil. Growing tips advance and branch, filaments meet and fuse into loops, and the web thickens toward the centre. Soft top light, slight sparkle on the threads. Locked camera.

### A52 · Snowflake / crystal growth
Start frame `a52-1.webp`. Macro time-lapse of a six-armed ice crystal growing on black. All six arms extend in sync from the centre, adding side branches in stages, with glittering refraction along the edges. Hold the finished crystal for the last two seconds. Locked camera.
