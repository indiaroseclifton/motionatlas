# AI cooking channel flowchart: cleaned up

`channel.mmd` is the cleaned source. Paste it into the flowchart studio (`/flowchart-studio`) or pick the "AI cooking channel" example there.

## What changed in the content
- **One start.** "Study Jean Phil lessons" now opens the chain, and "Pull top-views data" feeds the research step. Before, four steps had no way in.
- **Chef roster is a sequence.** You define the roster, then lock the photoreal identity, instead of the identity floating beside it.
- **Recipe source is a real decision.** "Recipe source?" branches into *Chef's own* and *Famous dish*. Only the famous branch goes through the rights check, and both meet at "Assign chef".
- **Voice sits in the line.** It runs between colour grade and sound design rather than hanging off the side.
- **The approval gate belongs to "Make the video".** A failed approval loops back to the shot list.
- **Memory belongs to "Money and feedback".** It sends two deliberate loops: *what worked* goes to the episode recipe, and *next round* goes back to research.
- **Stage names have no step numbers.** Every layout numbers the stages itself.
