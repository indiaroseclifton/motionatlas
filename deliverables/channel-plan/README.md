# AI cooking channel: plan pack

The charts behind the channel plan deck, drawn from the channel brief of 15 October 2026 in the channel's own styles.

| Chart | What it shows |
|---|---|
| `stages-*` | The loop: six stages, each step marked have / don't have, three loops back |
| `capability-*` | 8 of the loop's 30 steps in place, stage by stage |
| `cycle-*` | The same loop as a ring, for a single slide |
| `roadmap-*` | The brief's gaps in five workstreams over five phases (a proposed order, not dates) |
| `journey-*` | A viewer journey: a hypothesis to test with the first episodes |

- `*-noir` files are **Kitchen noir**, for video and slides.
- `*-paper` files are **Kitchen paper**, for documents and print.
- PNGs are 2×; SVGs scale freely and use Fraunces and DM Sans.

Counts: the loop has 30 steps, 8 in place. The brief's wider audit counts 40 parts, including each chef and the agent stack separately: 16 have, 24 don't.

To regenerate, serve the repository root and open `tools/channel-plan-charts.html?k=roadmap&s=paper`. `k` is one of stages, capability, cycle, roadmap or journey; `s` is noir, paper or any style id.
